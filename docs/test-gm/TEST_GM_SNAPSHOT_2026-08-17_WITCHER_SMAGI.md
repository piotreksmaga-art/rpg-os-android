## 2.4 Canon research — GM knowledge, not automatically Smagi knowledge
External Witcher-lore research performed 2026-08-17 to resolve uncertain taxonomy.

### Chaos, magic and Power — canonical operating model for TEST GM
Do NOT model Witcher magic as a generic internal mana bar.

In Witcher lore, `Chaos` is a valid description/aspect of magic, but magic is broader than a single fuel substance: it is also described as art and science. TEST GM must therefore avoid collapsing `magic`, `Chaos`, and `Power` into interchangeable RPG-resource labels.

For practical spellcasting, the important concept is `Power` (Siła): magical Power is drawn from forces/sources associated with nature and the four Primary Elements. A conservative operational model for GM reasoning is:

`SOURCE / ELEMENT -> DRAW POWER -> CONTROL / CHANNEL / SHAPE -> MAGIC / SPELL / EFFECT`

This is a GM conceptual model for canon-consistent narration, NOT a claim that the accepted RPG OS runtime currently implements a Witcher-specific energy subsystem.

### Primary Elements and source-versus-effect distinction
The four Primary Elements are Air, Earth, Fire and Water and may serve as sources of Power. The Element used as the source of Power is conceptually distinct from the visible effect produced. Therefore:
- moving water does NOT prove Power was drawn from Water;
- moving earth does NOT prove Power was drawn from Earth;
- manipulating air does NOT prove Power was drawn from Air;
- manipulating/enlarging flame does NOT prove Power was drawn from Fire;
- observed effects do NOT establish four affinities.

Different sources can differ in suitability/difficulty for a practitioner/effect. The campaign must discover Smagi's actual source-drawing behavior through evidence or formal teaching rather than retroactively assigning it from visible effects.

### Element tendencies relevant to future experiments
Canon/lore research supports treating Water as comparatively suitable for beginner drawing practice, Earth as containing great Power but potentially laborious to draw, Air as requiring skill, and Fire as especially powerful/dangerous. These are GM-side world-model expectations, not knowledge automatically possessed by Smagi.

Do NOT force experiment outcomes merely to match these expectations; campaign evidence still controls what becomes true for Smagi.

### Internal sensation / energy interpretation
Smagi's repeated subjective internal sensation during successful magical action is OBSERVATION. It must NOT be relabeled as `Chaos in the body`, `mana`, `internal energy pool`, or confirmed `Power sensing` without further evidence.

Permitted hypothesis: the sensation may correspond to one stage of drawing, channeling, controlling or shaping Power.

Future experiments may attempt to distinguish:

2. drawing Power without a visible effect, if Smagi can discover such a state;

4. stopping the effect/releasing control;
5. source proximity/environmental differences.

### Sources
A `Source` is not merely shorthand for `very talented mage`. It is a special canon category associated with innate sensitivity/ability involving Power and potentially instinctive/uncontrolled manifestations requiring training/control.

Smagi's unusually early spontaneous and increasingly controlled manifestations support monitoring:
`HYPOTHESIS: possible Source OR exceptional innate magical aptitude`.

They do NOT currently establish:
`FACT: Smagi is a Source`.

No Source-specific permanent mechanics, bonuses, potential value or diagnosis may be added solely from this hypothesis.

### Fire safety
Fire is especially dangerous as a source of Power, particularly for inexperienced practitioners and in Source-related contexts. Current candle experiments therefore remain meaningful safety evidence. However visible flame control is separate from proving Fire-source drawing.
