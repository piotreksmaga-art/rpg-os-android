## 2.4 Canon research — GM knowledge, not automatically Smagi knowledge
External Witcher-lore research performed 2026-08-17 to resolve uncertain taxonomy.

### Primary Elements and Power
Canon-derived/lore-supported model: magical Power can be drawn from four Primary Elements — Air, Earth, Fire and Water. The element used as a source does not necessarily equal the visible effect of the spell; a spell associated with one effect can in principle be powered from another source, though suitability/difficulty can differ. Therefore Smagi moving water, earth, air or flame does NOT by itself prove four affinities.

### Fire
Fire is especially powerful/dangerous as a source of Power and can overwhelm inexperienced users. Therefore current candle experiments remain important safety evidence, but they do not prove Smagi is drawing Power specifically from Fire; visible flame manipulation and source-of-Power selection must remain separate concepts.

### Sources
A `Source` is a special canon category for a person born with unusually strong natural magical ability whose uncontrolled manifestations can be dangerous and may require expert training. Smagi's early spontaneous/controlled manifestations make `possible Source or exceptional innate aptitude` a GM-side hypothesis worth monitoring, but current evidence is insufficient for CONFIRMED classification. No retroactive Source label is applied.

### Ban Ard
Ban Ard Academy is the principal male magical school in Kaedwen. Lore describes it as educating boys/young male adepts and providing formal magical education; later lore describes structured testing/curriculum. Thus Ban Ard is canonically appropriate as a possible education destination for Smagi. The campaign's existing application remains PENDING; canon suitability does not equal admission.

The campaign is currently in 1179. External Witcher sources combine book/game/reference material from multiple later dates.
